# Source and asset provenance

The GramClimate-specific interface, styles, synthetic dataset generator and numerical implementations were authored in this project. No other weather application's code, branding, imagery or dataset was imported. General algorithms such as PCA, K-Means and DBSCAN are established methods and are not claimed as new inventions.

The project is built on the Sites Vinext starter. Files in `components/ui`, `build`, supporting starter `scripts` and framework helpers originate from that starter and are not claimed as custom inventions. Dependencies include React, Vinext, Tailwind, Radix/shadcn primitives, Lucide icons and Sonner. These third-party components are copyrighted and governed by their licenses. See the generated dependency notice inventory and original packages. We do not claim that all source in the repository is free of third-party copyright.

The simple favicon and geometric plots are project-authored. No stock photos, third-party map tiles or remote fonts are used. Demo coordinates, weather values and panchayat labels are synthetic. They have no official or observational status.

The dependency notice inventory records installed package metadata and discovered local license/notice files. It is an inventory, not a legal opinion or a commercial-clearance certificate.
